# Limitations

Stated plainly, because several of them bound the headline result.

## 1. One graph, one organisation

Every partition re-partitions the **same** 708-service dependency graph from
**one** company. The 30 partitions are not independent systems: they share
services, so their results are positively correlated by an unknown amount.

**Consequence:** no confidence interval reported here has valid frequentist
coverage for a population of graphs, and no significance test was used as a
decision criterion. Conclusions are *conditional on this graph*. Partition-level
consistency (29/30) is evidence of robustness **to how the graph is split**, not
of generalisation to other organisations' systems.

## 2. Cascades are simulated, not observed

ChronoGraph publishes a dependency graph but **no propagation labels**. Every
cascade over the production topology is generated by `ml/synth.simulate_cascade`.
The structure is real; the failures are not.

**Consequence:** results isolate the effect of real topology. They are **not**
evidence of real-world failure-prediction accuracy. The only real propagation
evidence in the project is 22 incidents on a 19-node graph — too few to support
a general claim, and reported separately.

A second-order risk follows: the generator embeds assumptions (quorum nodes,
resilient nodes, retry amplification). Any model improvement measured against it
partly measures fit to those assumptions. We kept the quorum mechanism
specifically as an anti-circularity guard — the model provably cannot represent
it — and declined to "fix" that mismatch for exactly this reason.

## 3. Component attributes are synthetic

ChronoGraph publishes no component types or criticality. Types are inferred from
structural role within each subgraph; criticality is sampled from InfraTrace's
marginal **independently of degree**, so the attribute cannot manufacture a
signal predicting blast radius. **Only the wiring is real.**

## 4. The acceptance criteria were not met

The study failed 2 of 6 pre-registered checks (min partition gap −0.0122; ECE
regression +0.0938). It is reported as an **exploratory result with 30 usable
partitions**, not a successful validation. Thresholds were not relaxed after
seeing results.

## 4b. The margin over the strongest baseline is narrow

The strongest baseline is not logistic path features (0.5067) but a
hand-authored dependency-weight heuristic with its **eight weights fitted on
training data**, at PR-AUC 0.5379. SCP-GNN's lead over it is **+0.0141
(27/30 partitions)**, not the +0.0452 measured against logistic regression —
and that eight-cell heuristic is **better calibrated** (ECE 0.1204 vs 0.1316)
and **slightly more precise** (0.5073 vs 0.4977). Against the heuristic as
documented, without fitting, the lead is +0.0420 (29/30). A 1,441-parameter
model therefore buys 0.014 PR-AUC over nine interpretable numbers and gives
back calibration and precision to get it. See
[08-FIXED-WEIGHT-BASELINE.md](08-FIXED-WEIGHT-BASELINE.md).

## 5. Selection effects in the partition set

Of 32 accepted partitions, 2 (seeds 11, 25) were unusable because their
validation pool could not produce a single qualifying subgraph. The failure is
structural and was detected before any training, so it cannot bias the model
comparison — but the 30 analysed partitions are **conditioned on validation-pool
samplability** and are not a uniform random subset.

## 6. Hub presence is not hub preservation

Test pools reach global in-degree 73, but mean hub retention is **65%** — a hub
assigned to test keeps only the dependents not already claimed by a larger hub.
A 73-dependent hub observed with 36 retained dependents is a different
structural object. Per-hub retention is reported alongside every hub claim.

## 7. Subgraphs are not independent

Within a partition, TEST-R subgraphs overlap (mean pairwise Jaccard 0.46,
covering 147 of 212 test services). Inference is at the partition level, so this
cannot inflate degrees of freedom, but the 32 subgraphs per partition carry less
independent information than their count suggests.

## 8. Micro-averaged metrics

PR-AUC is pooled over decisions within a partition, so larger subgraphs
dominate. A macro-average over subgraphs would weight topologies equally and
could give different values. Raw predictions were retained for the `pos_weight`
arms only, so this cannot be recomputed for the main table without re-running.

## 9. The linear-link result is preliminary

The 28-parameter linear transmission function was compared to the 1,441-parameter
MLP in an **inline run with no saved artifact and no script**, on the *older*
substrate. It is not reproducible from the repository and has no stored per-seed
values. It must be labelled preliminary. See `00-CLAIMS-STATUS.md` B1.

## 10. No algorithmic novelty

SCP-GNN is the hyperparametric Independent Cascade model (Kalimeris et al. 2018;
applied to cascading failures by Xiang et al. 2024). The topology dependence we
observe is consistent with Fountoulakis et al. (2023). The contribution is the
database integration, the corrected evaluation and the negative results.
